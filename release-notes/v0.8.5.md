## Highlights

- Aura AI answers now appear as they are written, in the Aura rail and the composer's AI actions
- IMAP/SMTP accounts can sign in with a username that differs from the email address: **Sign in with → Username** adds the required login field, while the email address still provides the `From` header

## Fixes

- AI providers that always stream their responses no longer fail with "AI response was invalid"
