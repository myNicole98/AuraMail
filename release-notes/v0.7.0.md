## Highlights

- Contacts are now a full workspace: local contacts and CardDAV address books, with two-way sync, search, contact photos, and a detail pane
- Compose suggests contacts from your address books before falling back to mail history
- Right-click the sender in a message to send them a message, add or edit them as a contact, or copy their address
- Calendars can now be local too: every space gets an offline **Local** calendar, pinned above your CalDAV calendars
- Mail accounts get an **Identity name**, so recipients see `Name Surname <address>` instead of a bare address
- Settings has been rebuilt around a quieter, roomier layout with fewer boxes and segmented controls for short choices
- Light and dark themes were retuned for clearer surface hierarchy, readable secondary text, and a softer accent; the same hierarchy now applies to every theme, including Matugen
- The default splash uses the original AuraMail mark with its breathing animation; palette themes keep the four-ring animation

## Fixes

- In-app updates on Windows now replace the installed files safely and no longer restart the previous version
- Changing the wallpaper no longer repaints AuraMail when Matugen is off
- Saving a signature no longer clears the account's identity name
- The local calendar no longer shows a “secret not found” error when opening Calendar
- Contact suggestions no longer include your own addresses or hide a matching duplicate
- Deleting a space now also removes its calendar and contact credentials from the keyring
- The reader no longer shows the AuraMail logo when no message is selected
