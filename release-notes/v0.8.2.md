## Highlights

- Launch at login: **Settings → General** has a new **Startup** section that starts AuraMail with your computer, with a second switch to start hidden in the tray so no window appears at login (desktop only)
- Both switches read the real state from the operating system, so removing AuraMail from your login items by hand shows up as off
- The reader has a sun/moon button in its top-right corner that turns email dark mode on and off without opening Settings

## Fixes

- Email dark mode now follows its own setting in every theme; a light System or Matugen theme used to force message bodies light even with the setting on
- The login entry is written from the running app, so an AppImage update no longer leaves it pointing at the old mount path
